02 / Who we are

Professional support.
A practical approach.

Brington Group HSE Consultants is a Johannesburg-based consulting firm specialising in Occupational Health, Safety and Environmental Management.

We understand that safety compliance can be overwhelming. Our mission is to simplify it — by providing professional documentation, on-site support and expert guidance that keeps your project compliant, your workers safe, and your client happy.

We serve small contractors to large construction companies nationwide.
