Enquiry information

Your enquiry. Your choice to send.

The quote form helps you prepare a request for Brington Group HSE Consultants. It does not automatically submit your details.

What the form does

Your name, company, contact information and project details are used in your browser to prepare a message. This website does not send form entries to a server or save them in browser storage.

When you select “Continue to WhatsApp” or “Open email draft”, the prepared message is passed to your chosen app. You decide whether to send it. If you copy the message, it is placed on your device’s clipboard.

After you choose a contact option

Your WhatsApp or email provider handles the message under its own terms and privacy practices. A draft can remain in that app even if you do not send it. Please review the recipient and message before sending.

Do not include identity numbers, medical information, passwords or sensitive project documents in an initial enquiry. You can discuss any supporting documentation directly with Brington Group.

Website operation

The website uses locally hosted images and fonts and does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting service may process technical request information to deliver and protect the website. Private previews may also require a hosting-provider sign-in.
