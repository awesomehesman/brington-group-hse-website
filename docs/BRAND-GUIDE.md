# Brington Group brand guide

## Logo

Use the supplied BG emblem, not a marketing poster. The source in `assets/source/Logo Design.png` already contains the isolated emblem. Display it proportionally on white with clear space; never stretch, recolour or redraw it. `public/images/brington-emblem.webp` is the optimised website derivative. The footer provides a small white background for contrast. The wordmark beside it reads BRINGTON GROUP / HSE CONSULTANTS.

## Palette

- Corporate navy: `#002A54` — primary buttons, brand accents.
- Deep navy: `#061D35` — hero, trust section and footer.
- Gold: `#D5A13A` — primary hero CTA and restrained accents.
- Off-white: `#F5F3ED` — alternating content backgrounds.
- Ink: `#172C40`; body secondary: `#586776`.
- Silver-grey borders: `#DCE1E5`.

Gold text is used on dark backgrounds; darker ochre is used for small accents on light backgrounds. Do not put white body text on gold.

## Typography

Space Grotesk gives headings a technical, architectural character. DM Sans is used for readable body text, navigation and forms. Both are locally hosted with font-display swap. Headings use measured negative letter spacing. Small uppercase labels establish section hierarchy, but essential content stays in readable sentence case.

## Layout and components

The desktop content width is capped at 1280px. Generous section spacing reduces visual clutter. Service cards use thin borders, consistent numbering, Lucide icons and visible descriptions; links never depend on hover. Cards switch from four columns to two and then one. Hero CTAs and mobile form fields stack at narrow widths.

Buttons use a 52px minimum height with restrained corner rounding. Use navy for primary actions on light backgrounds, gold against navy, and a clear outline for secondary hero actions. Focus outlines are visible. Hover motion is slight and removed when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

## Photography

Use site inspections, PPE, scaffolding and construction details. Current stock photographs are illustrative and credited; they must not be described as Brington personnel or completed projects. Preserve enough navy overlay for hero text contrast. Owner-approved project photography can replace the stock images later.

## Voice and claims

Use clear, practical wording and the owner's service descriptions. Do not invent statistics, years in business, clients, registration or certification. Training availability and accreditation remain for confirmation, so the service invites visitors to discuss them rather than claiming accredited-provider status.
